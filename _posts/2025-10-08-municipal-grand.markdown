A 44-room hotel in Savannah, Georgia, housed in a former 1960 bank that later served as a local municipal building. One can find our logo in the Forsyth Park fountain and some of our words in Flannery O’Connor’s writings.
The project includes three food & beverage programs: Municipal Bar, The Sun Club, and The Hot Eye.

Deliverables: Naming, full visual identity, collateral and signage design, web design, print and signage production, brand voice, copywriting and editorial research.
